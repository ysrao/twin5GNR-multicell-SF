Cell A   Cell B   Cell C   White dots = Inter-tower HO region for the selected UE   Yellow squares = Intra-tower sector HO region (same tower, different sector)   ▲ = tower   ● = UE

How to read this: Colour = best server (strongest RSRP) at that point. White dots = handoff region, where the best and second-best cells are within 1 CQI of each other — a UE there would normally hand over, and ping-pong is possible. The trigger is the UE-reported CQI (quantised SINR, 0-15): the UE measures SINR on every sector, maps it to CQI, and reports it; the network hands over when a neighbour's CQI beats the serving cell's CQI. SINR here is the real one — serving RSRP against all other sectors' RSRP plus thermal noise — not the old 3 dB-below-serving assumption. Triangle = tower. Dot = UE, with its label. RSRP = EIRP − path loss, modelled. Capacity = bandwidth × log2(1+SINR) × MIMO streams (downlink streams capped at 8, uplink at 2 — the UE transmit limit), modelled, not measured throughput. Doppler = speed × frequency / speed of light, shown per UE.
The run on this page (Sionna ray trace at 3.6 GHz; street 1.5 m, 625 cells at 40 m):
  • Coverage (RSRP ≥ −90 dBm): 24.3%.
  • Median best-server RSRP: -105.7 dBm. 80.8% of cells have a ray-traced path in this run — the paths exist, but most are weak non-line-of-sight routes.
  • Provenance: run 14 Nov 2026 on a free Colab T4 GPU (Sionna RT 2.2.0, CUDA) on an open city scene built from the City of San Francisco’s published building footprints (DataSF, CC0; LiDAR ground and roof height per building) extruded as prisms over public terrain tiles, in local ENU metres about 37.801105, -122.404175; 110,396 triangles. The scene is redistributable and ships with the package.
  • Tower-B window: a 10 m grid over a 260 × 260 m window centred on tower B (x 120–380 m, y 120–380 m; 729 grid points) gives 99.7% of points with a path, median -75.9 dBm and 89.2% coverage ≥ -90 dBm on the streets around the site. The as-built antennas clear their local roofs by A +2.0 m, B +29.3 m and C +0.2 m; coverage within 150 m of each site is 13.0% (A), 91.1% (B) and 27.9% (C).
Assumptions, stated: Sionna sectors 90°/210°/330° in ENU. Foliage: not modelled. Sionna is a simulation on the open DataSF scene (LOD1 building prisms) with one material (ITU concrete) — it is not a measurement, and it does not include vehicles or indoor loss.

Assumptions, stated: RSRP on this page is the Sionna RT trace (per resource element); no formula path-loss model is used. Noise −174 dBm/Hz, noise figure 7 dB, SINR is computed per sector from all sectors' RSRP plus thermal noise; the UE reports it as CQI (0-15, ~2 dB steps from -6 dB, stated approx.), and handover is decided on CQI. ICI degradation, calculated (not assumed as a flat factor): ICI ratio = (π·f_D / Δf)² / 3, where f_D is Doppler and Δf is subcarrier spacing (30 kHz at 3.6 GHz). Effective SINR = 1 / (1/SINR + ICI ratio), for both DL and UL. DL uses the trace’s EIRP (47 dBm) and selected MIMO; UL uses UE power 23 dBm and MIMO capped at 2, so UL is usually lower. Each tower has 3 sectors at 90°, 210° and 330° (ENU), with the TR 38.901 pattern applied in the trace. UEs move in straight lines and bounce at the grid edge. The configuration is the paper’s (Table I); see the paper for method and limits.
